## 2. Printing Methods Offered

Useful to explain briefly on the site — customers often don't know the difference, and it signals expertise.

- **Offset Printing** — ink transferred via plates to a rubber blanket, then to paper. Best for large-volume jobs (invitation cards, bill books, brochures, books) where cost-per-piece drops at scale and color consistency matters.
- **Screen Printing** — ink pushed through a mesh stencil. Used for banners, cloth/bag printing, and jobs needing bold, durable, opaque color on varied surfaces.
- **Multicolor / Digital Printing** — direct digital output, no plates needed. Best for short runs, quick turnaround, variable data (e.g., ID cards with different names/photos), stickers, and proofs.

📝 Suggested framing: *"Whatever the job size — from a single ID card to a 5,000-copy invitation order — we have the right press for it."*
