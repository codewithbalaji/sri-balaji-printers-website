## Scope & visitor mode

Homepage (`app/page.tsx`) — Persuade. Light homepage that routes to deeper subpages (About/Services/Gallery/Contact), not a long one-page scroll.

## Audience, job, action, proof, constraints

Chennai/Tamil Nadu individuals and small businesses arriving with a specific print job in mind. Primary action: Call or WhatsApp (always reachable from the sticky header, repeated in hero and CTA band). Proof: 25+ years / since 2001, owner Dhanasekaran V, real Justdial 4.6★ (3 reviews) — never invent additional testimonials/reviews. No GST number displayed anywhere.

## Chosen direction

Category-standard execution (an unconventional kolam/dot-grid concept was offered via the new-work direction roll and explicitly declined by the client in favor of the familiar local-business template). Distinction comes from craft and a committed CMYK-inspired palette (magenta primary, cyan secondary, yellow highlight — see DESIGN.md), not from structure. Hero visual is an illustrative "print sample stack" mockup (rounded card shapes, no real photography) standing in for a placeholder photo until real work photos arrive.

## Memorable moment

The magenta "See everything we print" tile capping the service-category grid — turns the routing action itself into the visually loudest element on the page.

## Unresolved decisions

- Service category teasers currently all link to `/services` (no anchors yet since that page doesn't exist).
- About/Services/Gallery/Contact pages are out of scope for this brief and still need their own shape/build passes.
