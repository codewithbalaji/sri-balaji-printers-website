## Platform

## Users

- **Individuals** planning weddings and personal functions: invitation cards, thamboolam (wedding return-gift bags), banners for functions.
- **Small & medium businesses** in Ambattur/Chennai and across Tamil Nadu: bill books, visiting cards, ID cards, brochures, stickers.
- **Event organizers**: banners for weddings, functions, and commercial promotions.
- **Institutions/publishers** (lower volume, occasional): books, catalogs.

All segments are typically searching for a local, reliable printing press that can handle their specific job without needing to compare printing methods themselves — they come with a job in mind, not a printing-technique preference.

## Product Purpose

Sri Balaji Printers is a commercial printing press in Ambattur, Chennai, run by proprietor Dhanasekaran V (25+ years in the printing trade; business established 2001). The website exists to convert local search/word-of-mouth interest into calls, WhatsApp messages, or walk-ins for print jobs — from a single visiting card to a bulk wedding order.

Success = a visitor quickly finds the service they need, trusts the shop's credibility (experience, reviews, address/landmark), and contacts via call/WhatsApp.

## Positioning

One shop offering three printing methods under one roof — offset, screen, and digital/multicolor — so customers don't need to go elsewhere for different job types or volumes. 25+ years of standing at the same Ambattur location is the trust anchor. The regional specialty (thamboolam/wedding return-gift bag printing) is a distinct, locally-searched service most generic printing sites don't call out.

**Caution:** other unrelated businesses with the same/similar name exist in Coimbatore, Bangalore, Tirupati, and Thanjavur. Do not reuse their history, "since 19XX" claims, or reviews — only the Ambattur/Venkatapuram (No. 11B, South Park Street, opp. Vijaya Bank & Naturals) listing is this business.

## Capabilities and Constraints

**Printing methods offered:**
- Offset printing — large-volume jobs (invitation cards, bill books, brochures, books), cost drops at scale, consistent color.
- Screen printing — banners, cloth/bag printing, bold/durable/opaque color on varied surfaces.
- Multicolor/digital printing — short runs, quick turnaround, variable data (e.g. ID cards), stickers, proofs.

**Products & services:**
- Wedding & event: wedding invitation cards, birthday/function invitations, wedding banners, event banners, thamboolam/wedding return-gift bags (custom names/date/motif).
- Business & corporate: visiting/business cards, bill books (multi-copy, often carbonless), ID cards, brochures, catalogs, books.
- Banners & signage: commercial, promotional/advertisement, flex banners.
- Stickers & labels: all types offered (product labels, vinyl, decorative, etc.).

**Explicitly not offered:** newspaper printing (different process/scale) — omit rather than draw attention to it; a one-line note is optional if the client later asks for it.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with breadth (offset + screen + digital under one roof) and longevity (25+ years, since 2001) as the core trust argument.
2. Make it effortless to act — call/WhatsApp CTA should be reachable from anywhere on the site, since the buying decision path is a phone call or walk-in, not checkout.
3. Give the regional specialty (thamboolam bags) its own visible identity rather than burying it inside a generic "bags" category — it's a distinct local search term.
4. Never borrow credibility (history, claims, reviews) from unrelated same-named businesses elsewhere in Tamil Nadu/Karnataka/Andhra.
5. Keep service-area messaging accurate: statewide order-taking, not walk-in-only.

No product-specific accessibility requirement has been established. Given the local, phone-first customer base (many contacting via call/WhatsApp rather than web forms), ensure phone/WhatsApp contact affordances are prominent and usable without relying on JavaScript-heavy interactions.
